The **Watchdog** page is the triage surface for risk findings. [Guardrail policies](/docs/ai-control-plane/secure/guardrails) record a finding for every match in an agent session. Watchdog clusters related findings into signals and ranks them by severity. A signal groups findings that share a root cause, such as one detection rule firing repeatedly for the same team. Triaging a short list of ranked signals is faster than reading thousands of raw findings.

This page covers the triage loop, in order:

Open the page from **Security and Policy > Watchdog** in the dashboard.

<Callout type="info">
  Viewing this page requires the `org:admin` scope.
</Callout>

The raw finding-level log lives on [Risk Events](/docs/ai-control-plane/secure/risk-events). Watchdog replaces the former Risk Overview page, and old Risk Overview links redirect here.

## Reading the dashboard

![The Watchdog dashboard with headline metric cards, the exposure-by-data-type bar, and the ranked active signals list](/assets/docs/ai-control-plane/secure/watchdog-dashboard.webp)

Set the time range with the picker above the cards.

Four headline cards compare the selected time range against the previous period:

- **Org risk score**: a weighted score of open risk, driven up by critical signals
- **Findings**: detection rule matches in the range
- **Open signals**: active signal clusters, with a count of how many are critical
- **Users exposed**: users whose sessions produced findings

The exposure bar breaks findings down by data type, such as secrets or PII. Each slice is also a filter: select a data type to narrow the signal list, and select it again to clear.

The **Active signals** list contains every open signal in the range. Each row shows the signal's severity score, the rule behind it, the apps it was observed in, a trend sparkline, and the count of affected users and teams. Group the list by **Severity**, **Data type**, **Team**, or **App**, and filter by severity level.

## Investigating a signal

1. Select a signal in the **Active signals** list. The signal drawer opens.

   ![The signal drawer showing severity, user and finding counts, top affected users, and redacted evidence with a reveal control and per-finding Exclude action](/assets/docs/ai-control-plane/secure/watchdog-signal-drawer.webp)

2. Review the evidence. Matched content is redacted by default: select **Click to reveal** on a match to reveal it in place, or **Reveal all** above the evidence list to reveal every match.

   ![The signal drawer's evidence list with the Reveal all toggle and a match's Click to reveal control highlighted](/assets/docs/ai-control-plane/secure/watchdog-reveal-evidence.webp)

<Callout type="info">
  Every reveal is recorded in the [audit log](/docs/ai-control-plane/org-admin/audit-logs).
</Callout>

The drawer's regions, top to bottom:

- The header shows the severity, the rule description, and the signal's first and last occurrence.
- Summary cards count the affected users, findings, and teams, with the trend for the window.
- **Top affected users** lists who triggered the findings and how often.
- **Evidence** lists the findings, each with its message context and the rule that matched.

Findings from prompt-based policies show the judge's rationale in place of a redacted match, so their evidence carries no reveal controls. To investigate a single finding across its full session, use [Risk Events](/docs/ai-control-plane/secure/risk-events), the finding-level log.

## Resolving signals

1. Select one or more signals with the checkboxes in the **Active signals** list, then open the **Suppress** menu.

   ![The active signals list with two signals selected and the Suppress menu open, showing Suppress Once and Create Rule](/assets/docs/ai-control-plane/secure/watchdog-bulk-actions.webp)

2. Choose the action:
   - **Suppress Once**: dismisses the selected findings from the active list, the score, and the headline counts. It changes no policy, and a suppressed finding can be restored. Use it for noise that is not expected to recur
   - **Create Rule**: creates an [exclusion rule](/docs/ai-control-plane/secure/exclusions) from the selection, suppressing its findings retroactively and going forward. Use it for false positives that recur. The rule criteria prefill from the signal

The drawer suppresses a single signal with the same menu.

The **Suppressed** section below the active list holds everything the signal clusters no longer count, excluded from the score and the headline counts. Each row shows why: a manually suppressed finding offers **Restore**, and a rule-suppressed finding offers **View rule**.

Findings from prompt-based policies carry a judged verdict rather than a reproducible match, so tune those policies through the [guardrail](/docs/ai-control-plane/secure/guardrails) and its detection scope rather than value-based exclusions.

For signals that reflect real risk, remediation happens outside Watchdog: rotate the exposed credential, block the server, or tighten [role grants](/docs/ai-control-plane/org-admin/roles-and-permissions). To stop the behavior rather than report it, raise the policy's action from flag to [deny or quarantine](/docs/ai-control-plane/secure/guardrails#action-severity-and-audience).
